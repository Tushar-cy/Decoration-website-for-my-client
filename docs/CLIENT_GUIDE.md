# Decor Joy Gurgaon — Owner's Guide

> Plain-language guide for the owner. No tech jargon. Everything you need to run the business from your phone or laptop.

---

## Getting to the Admin Panel

Go to: **https://decorjoygurgaon.com/admin**

Login with your email and password. If you forget your password, ask your tech support to reset it.

---

## 1. Adding a New Decoration Package (Product)

1. In the left sidebar, tap **Products**
2. Tap the **+ Add Product** button (top right)
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Title** — e.g. "Chrome Gold Balloon Ring Arch"
   - **Category** — choose from your existing categories (Birthday, Anniversary, etc.)
   - **Description** — describe what's included in the package
   - **Price** — enter the price in Rupees (the system converts to paise automatically)
   - **Photos** — upload images; you can drag and drop or click "Upload Image"
   - **Active** — tick this for the product to appear on the website
4. Tap **Save Product**

**Tips:**
- Set **Sort Order** to control which products appear first (lower number = first)
- You can **Duplicate** an existing product to save time on similar packages
- To temporarily hide a product without deleting it, untick "Active" and save

---

## 2. Blocking a Date (No Bookings That Day)

1. In the sidebar, tap **Slot Availability**
2. You'll see a calendar view of the current month
3. Click on the date you want to block (e.g. a holiday or personal day)
4. A toggle appears — click **Block This Date**
5. The date turns red on the calendar

**To unblock:** Click the red date again and tap **Unblock**

Customers will not be able to select a blocked date when placing an order.

---

## 3. Handling an Order

When a new order comes in, you'll get a **WhatsApp notification** on your phone.

### Finding the order:
1. Tap **Orders** in the sidebar
2. New orders are at the top (or filter by "Pending")
3. Click on the order to open the details drawer

### What you'll see:
- Customer name, phone, event date and time slot
- Full address with locality and pincode
- Items ordered with variants
- Advance paid (or "Pay on Confirmation" if payment was offline)

### Confirming an order:
1. Open the order details
2. Under **Status**, move it from **Pending** to **Confirmed**
3. Add a note if needed (e.g. "Called customer, confirmed setup time 10 AM")
4. Click **Save**

The customer will receive an email/SMS confirmation automatically.

### Order Status Guide:

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Pending | Just placed, not yet confirmed |
| Confirmed | You've confirmed it and called the customer |
| In Progress | Setup team is on the way / setting up |
| Completed | Setup done, job finished |
| Cancelled | Order was cancelled |

### If a customer wants to cancel or reschedule:
- Open the order → find the **Reschedule** button to pick a new date/slot
- Or click **Cancel** and choose whether to issue a refund (owner only)

## 4. Purpose Form Submissions (Inquiries)

When someone fills out the "Plan My Event" form on the website, you'll get a **WhatsApp ping** immediately. They also get a WhatsApp button to message you directly.

### Viewing submissions:
1. Tap **Submissions Inbox** in the sidebar
2. Each card shows the customer's name, phone, event type, and their answers
3. Click a card to see the full submission

### Taking action:
- **Status** column shows: New → Reviewing → Quoted → Converted → Closed
- Update the status as you work through the inquiry
- Call the customer on the phone shown in the submission
- Once they place an order, mark the submission as **Converted**

---

## 5. Gallery — Adding New Photos

After every successful event, add photos to your portfolio.

1. Tap **Gallery** in the sidebar
2. Tap **Add Photo** (or **Bulk Upload** to add many at once)
3. Upload the photo (it goes to Cloudinary automatically)
4. Fill in:
   - **Title** — brief description (e.g. "Chrome Gold Arch — Sector 57 Birthday")
   - **Category** — Birthday, Anniversary, etc.
   - **Caption** — location or event detail (e.g. "DLF Phase 4, Gurgaon")
5. Tick **Featured on Homepage** to show it in the top section
6. Tick **Visible on Site** to publish it
7. Save

**Bulk Upload:** Click the "Bulk Upload" button, select multiple photos at once. They all upload in sequence. You can then edit each one to add titles and categories.

---

## 6. Customer Reviews (Testimonials)

1. Tap **Testimonials** in the sidebar
2. Tap **Add Review**
3. Enter:
   - Customer name
   - Location (e.g. Sushant Lok, Gurgaon)
   - Event type (e.g. 1st Birthday Party)
   - Their review text
   - Star rating (1–5)
4. Tick **Show on website**
5. Save

To temporarily hide a review: click the **Visible / Hidden** toggle. It stays in your system but won't show on the website.

The dashboard shows you everything at a glance.

| Section | What it tells you |
|---------|------------------|
| Today's Setups | Orders that are set up today — with address and customer phone |

## 8. Emergency Controls (Operational Switches)

These are in **Settings** (owner only). They take effect **within 60 seconds** — no tech help needed.

### Turning off online payments temporarily
If Razorpay has an issue and customers can't pay online:

1. Go to **Settings**
2. Under "Operational Switches", **uncheck** "Online Payments Enabled"
3. Click **Save Settings**

Customers will now see: *"Online payment is temporarily unavailable. Our team will call you to confirm this booking within 2 hours."* They can still place the order — you confirm it manually.

### Pausing all new bookings
If your team is fully booked or away:

1. Settings → tick **"Pause New Bookings"**
2. Save

New orders will be blocked with a friendly message telling customers to WhatsApp you.

### Showing an announcement banner
For Diwali closures, special offers, etc.:

1. Settings → type your message in **"Site-Wide Announcement Banner"**
2. e.g. `Closed 12–15 Oct for Diwali. Accepting bookings from 16 Oct.`
3. Save

A yellow banner will appear at the top of your website. Delete the text and save to remove it.

## 9. Useful Tips

- **Your website auto-saves product photos** to Cloudinary. You don't need to manage file storage.
- **All prices are set by you on the server** — customers cannot change prices.
- **Slots fill up automatically** — once a slot is full, customers can't book it.
- **WhatsApp notifications** come to your phone for every new inquiry and order.
- If a customer contacts you directly on WhatsApp, you can still create the order manually from the admin orders panel.
